About SASH MARINES

A professionally managed ship supply and marine services company committed to excellence.

Built on a strong foundation of generational family experience in shipping supply, we offer proven industry knowledge, trust, and operational excellence. We understand the critical importance of timely delivery, uncompromised quality, and dependable service in marine operations, allowing us to anticipate vessel requirements and provide customized solutions meeting international standards. We specialize in supplying ship provisions, bonded stores, marine equipment, spare parts, safety items, and all essential vessel requirements. Our highly responsive team and strong logistics network ensure fast turnaround at ports and anchorages. Integrity, transparency, and customer satisfaction are our core values. We strive to build long-term partnerships with ship owners and managers by providing consistent quality, competitive pricing, and round-the-clock support. Driven by legacy and powered by innovation, SASH Marines is your trusted marine supply partner, ensuring vessels sail safely and efficiently.

Comprehensive Marine Solutions

Everything your vessel needs, delivered.

Ship Provisions Supply

We supply fresh and dry crew provisions, including produce, frozen proteins (meat, seafood, poultry), groceries, dairy, beverages, and water.

Bonded Stores Supply

Duty-free consumables for vessels include cigarettes and tobacco; spirits, wines, and soft drinks; confectionery and chocolates; and perfumes and personal care items.

Cabin Stores & Crew Welfare Items

We provide daily-use crew comfort items, including bedding, linens, towels, toiletries, hygiene supplies, kitchen utensils, cleaning tools, and laundry chemicals.

Deck & Engine Stores

We supply technical marine items, including ropes, mooring lines, wires, paints, thinners, coatings, welding equipment, consumables, fasteners (bolts, nuts), valves, gaskets, lubricants, oils, and greases.

Safety & Fire-Fighting Equipment

We supply essential safety equipment, including life jackets, life buoys, fire extinguishers, fire hoses, nozzles, breathing apparatus, emergency lights, and safety signs.

Marine Spare Parts Supply

We supply critical machinery spares, including engine parts, pumps, compressors, filters, electrical spares, control panels, and mechanical tools and equipment.

Fresh Water & Drinking Water Supply

We supply vessels with potable water and necessary tank cleaning chemicals.

Medical Stores & First Aid Supplies

We supply medical provisions, including the ship medical chest, emergency medicines, and first-aid kits.

Logistic & Delivery Services at Port

Our service ensures on-time onboard and anchorage delivery, port clearance coordination, and 24/7 emergency supply.

Waste Management & Sludge Disposal

We offer environmental compliance services, including garbage removal, sludge and bilge disposal, and full MARPOL compliance.
